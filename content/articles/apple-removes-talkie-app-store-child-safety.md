Apple has removed Talkie, an AI companion app developed by MiniMax, from the App Store worldwide following an investigation into harmful interactions between its virtual characters and children.

The decision follows reporting by [Australia's ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-25/chinese-ai-companion-app-talkie-child-harm-allegations/107416128) and the [Bureau of Investigative Journalism](https://www.thebureauinvestigates.com/stories/2026-11-24/talkie-china-chatbot-children). Apple cited sexual content as the reason for removal, without establishing that its decision specifically concerned messages delivered to minors.

## How children accessed an adult-rated app

Talkie allows users to interact with fictional AI characters. Unlike scripted entertainment, the conversations are generated in response to individual messages.

The investigation raised concerns about children accessing the service despite its adult age rating. Self-declared ages and marketplace content ratings are not equivalent to effective age verification.

Australia's [eSafety Commissioner](https://www.esafety.gov.au/research/talking-to-machines-childrens-experiences-with-ai-assistants-and-companions) surveyed 1,950 children aged 10 to 17 about their use of AI. Among respondents who had used an AI assistant or companion, 54% reported personal or social uses and 20% reported seeking advice about mental health or wellbeing. These figures describe wider AI use, not Talkie specifically.

## Apple is tightening its age-rating requirements

Apple's [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) set requirements for inappropriate content and user protection. Generative AI introduces an additional challenge: a chatbot can produce new content after initial app review.

Apple's [November update to its developer questionnaire](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yxkh08us) introduces declarations for dating and relationship features, including virtual companions that participate in romantic or sexual conversations. Developers must complete the declarations by January 12, 2027.

An age rating describes the suitability of an application. Age assurance is a separate process intended to establish whether users meet access requirements.

## A chatbot with millions of users

[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/apple-pulls-minimax-ai-chatbot-app-store-over-child-safety-concerns-2026-11-26/) reported that Talkie had about 11 million monthly active users across its app and website in October, citing research firm Unique. ABC also reported that Apple had removed a previous version in 2024 and that MiniMax introduced a replacement in 2025.

## What the removal means for existing users

Removing an app from Apple's marketplace does not automatically uninstall existing copies or shut down the underlying online service. The app was reported to remain available on Google Play on Wednesday. Its current availability has not been independently confirmed, and plans for reinstatement remain unclear.

## FAQ

### Does App Store removal automatically delete Talkie from existing devices?

No. Marketplace removal is distinct from uninstalling an existing application or shutting down the service.
