Google introduced the Keep Activity name in 2025. Older guides may call the switch Gemini Apps Activity; that name also remains on the page where you manage recorded activity. [Google’s announcement](<https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/temporary-chats-privacy-controls/>)

## The practical comparison {#practical-comparison}

Here, “normal chat” means an ordinary conversation with Keep Activity enabled, not a claim about every account’s default.

| Question | Normal chat: activity on | Ordinary chat: activity off | Temporary Chat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What does this choice cover? | Ordinary conversations while enabled | Future ordinary conversations until changed | The temporary conversation |
| Saved to Gemini Apps Activity? | Yes | No, for new conversations | No |
| Available as saved chat history? | Yes, until deleted | New conversations are not saved as history | No; unavailable after you leave |
| Routine chat retention | Your deletion/auto-delete settings | Up to 72 hours | Up to 72 hours |
| AI model improvement/training | May be used\* | Excluded unless feedback is submitted\* | Chats excluded\* |
| Memory of earlier chats | Available when eligible and enabled | Unavailable | No personalised responses or new information saved for personalisation |
| Response feedback | Available | Available; see exception below | Not available in current help |
| Removes older saved conversations? | No | Only if you also choose deletion | No |

*The training row is not a promise of zero processing or zero human access. See the exceptions below.*

The comparison follows Google’s [activity guide](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892>), [Temporary Chat instructions](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745?hl=en>), [Memory requirements](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598469>), [feedback guide](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275746>) and [Privacy Hub](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en>).

## History: what you can return to {#history}

History is the collection you can open again. For personal accounts, Google’s tools for finding and managing saved chats require activity saving to be enabled. Switching it off changes that experience; an absent conversation list is not proof that all older records were deleted. [Google’s chat-history guide](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13666746>)

A temporary conversation is deliberately disposable from your perspective. Google says you cannot return to it after leaving. If you need a packing list or a useful explanation later, copy the part you need before closing it. Remember that your copy then has its own storage location. [Temporary Chat help](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745?hl=en>)

For example, a one-off question about a surprise gift suits a temporary session. A project you plan to revisit needs a deliberate record-keeping choice.

## Retention: what may remain after a chat disappears {#retention}

With activity saving enabled, the standard auto-delete interval is **18 months**. You can select three months, 36 months or no automatic deletion, and delete records yourself. [Google’s activity controls](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892>)

Google’s current Privacy Hub says **Temporary Chats and chats created while Keep Activity is off are retained with your account for 72 hours**. It also says Gemini can use **the last 24 hours of the chat as context when responding**. The 24-hour figure describes the context available for replies; it is not the retention period. [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en>)

**Treat 72 hours as the documented short-term chat window, not an all-data erasure guarantee.** Google’s broader retention policy allows longer storage for purposes such as security and legal obligations, and explains that removing data from view and completing deletion are different stages. [Google’s retention policy](<https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention>)

There is also a separate retention rule for material already reviewed by people, explained below.

## Model improvement and training {#model-improvement}

With activity enabled, Google can use saved activity to develop services and train AI models. With it disabled, future chats are excluded from model improvement unless you submit feedback. [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en>)

Temporary conversations are excluded from AI-model training under Google’s stated rules. [Temporary Chat announcement](<https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/temporary-chats-privacy-controls/>)

**Exception — other improvement processing:** Google says Gemini settings do not control processing chats into anonymised data for service improvement. The training exclusions therefore should not be read as excluding every improvement-related use. [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en>)

Google says audio and Gemini Live video/screenshare recordings aren’t used to improve its services by default, and their use for improvement is controlled separately. Do not assume one switch describes every type of media. [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en>), [Google’s recording controls](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892>)

Training changes the system; personalisation changes how it responds to you. Those are separate controls.

## Personalisation: Memory and saved instructions {#personalisation}

Gemini’s Memory feature can draw on earlier conversations. It requires activity saving to be enabled, along with an eligible personal account. Turning Memory off is a narrower choice if your aim is to stop references to past chats while retaining a searchable history. It does not, by itself, switch off the separate activity control. [Google’s Memory guide](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598469>)

Temporary Chat has a different boundary: Google’s current help says it does not provide personalised responses or save information for future personalisation. [Temporary Chat help](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745?hl=en>)

Also check **Instructions for Gemini**, called Saved info in some interfaces. A preference you deliberately saved is a separate item you can inspect, switch off, edit or delete. Do not use an empty chat list as your audit of those instructions. [Google’s instructions guide](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598625>)

A useful distinction is:

- “Stop using my previous conversations” → check Memory.
- “Remove that preference I saved” → check your instructions.
- “Keep this one conversation out of future personalisation” → start a temporary session.

## Feedback and human review: the exceptions {#feedback-human-review}

**Feedback can include the conversation.** A response rating or report can carry more than your written comment: Google’s feedback guide includes associated prompts and answers, plus relevant uploaded or connected-app content. Read the submission details before sending it. [Google’s feedback documentation](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275746>)

For activity-off chats, that matters because of the training exception above. Current Temporary Chat instructions say response feedback is unavailable; do not assume the same feedback controls exist in both modes. [Temporary Chat help](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745?hl=en>)

**Safety review remains possible.** Google may use human reviewers for protection and safety even in these modes. Reviewed chats and related data, including reviewed feedback, can be retained for up to three years after being disconnected from your account. Deleting your activity does not remove those copies. [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en>)

“Disconnected from your account” describes how a record is handled. It does not make a name or address you typed into the conversation harmless to share.

## What happens to older conversations? {#older-conversations}

Switching off future saving does not itself clear your previous history. Google offers separate choices to disable saving alone or combine that change with deletion. You can also delete selected periods or individual records. [Google’s activity management guide](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892>)

If an old topic is affecting responses, Google says deleting the relevant conversation can stop its use for personalisation, although there may be a short delay. Review deliberately saved instructions separately. [Memory guidance](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598469>), [instructions guidance](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598625>)

Records in other Google services are separate: deleting Gemini activity does not delete those services’ data. [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en>)

## Which option should you use? {#which-option}

These are DECODISTA’s practical recommendations, based on the differences above.

| If your goal is… | Choose or check… |
| --- | --- |
| Ask one unrelated question without shaping later conversations | Temporary Chat |
| Avoid building a saved history during ordinary daily use | Keep Activity off |
| Keep a project history but stop references to earlier chats | Leave activity saving on; switch Memory off |
| Remove something you already discussed | Delete the relevant activity; check saved instructions |
| Report a bad answer without sharing unexpected context | Inspect the feedback disclosure before submitting |
| Keep confidential work material within approved controls | Check the organisation’s permitted account and retention rules first |

Before typing, ask yourself:

1. **Which account am I using?** Confirm personal versus organisation-managed.
2. **Will I need this conversation later?** Decide how you will retain the useful output.
3. **Do I want it influencing later replies?** Choose the appropriate history and Memory controls.
4. **Does the prompt contain unnecessary identifiers?** Remove details the task does not need.

To find the controls on the web, Google documents the temporary-session button beside **New chat**, and the activity page under **Settings & help → Activity**. Use these as navigation pointers; labels can change. [Usage help](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745?hl=en>), [activity help](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892>)

## Work and school accounts: check a different set of rules {#work-school-accounts}

**Do not apply the personal-account table to an employer or school account.** Your Workspace edition determines the applicable protections, and an administrator can control conversation retention. Some accounts receive Gemini as an additional service rather than under the same protections as a qualifying core Workspace service. [Google’s managed-account guide](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14620100>)

For qualifying Workspace use, Google states separate restrictions on human review and on using customer content for generative-AI training outside the organisation without permission. Those protections do not amount to a promise that the organisation itself cannot retain or access records. [Workspace Privacy Hub](<https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub>)

**Documentation transparency:** Google’s current consumer Gemini help says Temporary Chat is unavailable for work and school accounts, while its Workspace administrator guide, updated **10 September 2026**, documents administrator-controlled Temporary Chats for organisations. These pages differ; availability and retention depend on the managed environment and administrator configuration. [Consumer Gemini help](<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745?hl=en>), [Workspace administrator guide](<https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/gemini-app/enable-temporary-chats-in-gemini-app>)

Where Google Vault retention rules apply, temporary or deleted conversations can still be retained and accessible to authorised administrators or Vault users. Ask your administrator which rules cover your account. [Workspace Temporary Chat and deletion controls](<https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/gemini-app/enable-temporary-chats-in-gemini-app>)
