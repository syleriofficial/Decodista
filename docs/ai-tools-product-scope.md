# Decodista AI Tools — Product-only scope

## Product definition
AI Tools is a **global English-first discovery and comparison application**, not a blog or editorial article category.

### Product routes allowed
- `/ai-tools` — searchable Tools directory, category filters and task Finder.
- `/ai-tools/[slug]` — structured tool profiles with provider and evidence links.
- `/ai-tools/compare?first=...&second=...` — side-by-side, factually sourced comparison.
- `/ai-tools/pricing` — sourced and dated pricing records; never invent regional costs.
- `/ai-tools/alternatives?tool=...` — related alternatives without unsupported rankings.

### Excluded from this product
- **No editorial guides, tutorial articles, How-To blog posts, PDF walkthroughs or blog archives under `/ai-tools`.**
- No fake testimonials, invented test scores, winner badges, real-time price claims or sponsored placements presented as organic rankings.
- Do not promote AI Tools blog content on the Decodista editorial homepage or news-category pages.
- Existing news and How-To sections outside `/ai-tools` are separate and are not changed by this rule.

### Evidence and SEO
- Display each tool's official source, evidence date and limitations.
- Keep introductory/thin URLs `noindex` until they offer independently reviewable substantive product value; never claim indexing from successful CI alone.
- Removed editorial pages must not appear in navigation or the XML sitemap.
- Privacy-first analytics: consent-gated, no personal queries in events.

### Product work roadmap
1. Clean editorial-only code and tests while preserving actual tools, comparisons and search.
2. Improve on-page search, sorting and use-case-based decision support.
3. Build structured feature and plan comparisons, then expand the catalog with source verification.
4. Add privacy-conscious Saved Tools and meaningful user utilities, not content farms.
5. Measure product-only traffic and task completion; ten million monthly pageviews is a long-term goal, not a promise.

Do not change site navigation, unrelated news posts or production analytics merely to meet these product goals.
